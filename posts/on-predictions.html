On Predictions

Most predicitions are useless, as Nate Silver and Taleb like to argue. It appears that the world is simply too complex for one to make sufficiently precise predicitions about most things that matter to us.

Still, there is something appealing about committing to a prediction before the outcome is known. So I’m keeping a record of them here, mostly to see how they age.

Prediction Log

  1. 14/09/2026 - 21:47 — Augmented reality will remain a niche technology through 2030. Meta glasses and similar devices will not break through with the general public: privacy concerns will persist, and they largely solve a problem that does not exist.
  2. 14/09/2026 - 21:50 — LLMs are not the path to AGI, although I am not convinced by any of the current alternatives.
