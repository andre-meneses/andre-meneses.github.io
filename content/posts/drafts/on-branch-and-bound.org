#+TITLE: On heuristics ?
#+DATE: <2025-05-19 Mon>
#+AUTHOR: André Meneses
#+FILETAGS: :branch-and-bound:heurisitics:complexity:
#+DESCRIPTION: 

- Finding an optimal decision tree is a NP-Complete problem, one must rely on a local, branch to branch optimization and hope that the resulting tree works well enough. 

- There is a dimension where optimal solutions live but remain mostly unacessible, we can see shadows and glimpses by trying to peak over the walls but they live extremely well guarded.
  We can very rarely break-in but we are quickly reminded that we belong outside.  

- There is something deeply platonic about heuristics. 

- We can model what to study as an optmization problem

- The struggle between depth and breadth seem quite universal, a*, branch and bound, specialist x generalist
